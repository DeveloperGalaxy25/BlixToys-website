THE BLIX APPROACH

Two decades of turning ideas into play.

Blix brings together product creativity and hands-on manufacturing experience. From raw materials and product development to moulding, finishing, assembly, packing and dispatch, we work across the journey that takes an idea to a finished product.

Our focus is to make play more joyful for children and everyday life more reassuring for parents—with products that balance thoughtful design, useful function, durability and accessible value.

OUR VISION

To become a trusted name in play, everywhere.

We aspire to be a globally loved and trusted children’s products brand—bringing safety-conscious thinking, thoughtful design and affordability together for modern families.

OUR MISSION

Make parenting easier. Make childhood more joyful.

We create safe, stylish and functional products for every stage from newborn to eight years, combining globally inspired design with the needs of Indian homes and quality built to last.
